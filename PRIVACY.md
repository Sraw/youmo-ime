## 隐私政策

幽默输入法不收集任何数据，也不上传给我们或任何第三方。

幽默输入法不申请网络权限，无法联网，也不发通知。它有两个版本：

- **文字版**（默认）：只申请振动权限，用于按键振动。
- **语音版**：多申请一个录音权限，只在你按住说话时录音；语音在手机上识别，录音不保存、不发送。

两个版本都是：

- 拼音解码、两个句子模型都在手机上运行。
- 你输入的内容、学到的词和词频、剪贴板记录、设置，都只存在手机上这个应用自己的目录里。
- 为了按前文给出更准的候选，引擎会读输入框里光标前的文字，和你打的字一样只在手机上处理。密码框等敏感输入框不读。
- 只有你主动导出时（"高级"里的备份全部，用户词库、自定义短语和主题的导出，日志和堆内存转储），这些数据才会写成文件，存到你选的位置。
- 例外是 Android 12 及以上系统自带的换机迁移（新旧手机直连）：系统会把设置、学到和添加的词、自定义短语、导入的词库和主题一起搬到新手机，剪贴板记录不会。应用不使用云端备份。
- 卸载应用会删除它的全部数据。

本政策如有变动，会在这个文件里更新。

## Privacy Policy

Youmo IME collects no data, and uploads none to us or to anyone else.

Youmo IME asks for no network permission and cannot go online; it posts no notifications either. There are two builds:

- **Text** (the default): asks only to vibrate, for key feedback.
- **Voice**: asks for the microphone as well, used only while you hold to talk; speech is recognized on the phone, and the
  recording is neither kept nor sent.

In both:

- Pinyin decoding and both sentence models run on the phone.
- What you type, the words and frequencies it learns, the clipboard history and the settings stay in the app's own directories on the phone.
- To suggest better candidates from what comes before, the engine reads the text before the cursor in the input field. Like what you type, it is handled on the phone only, and it is not read from password or other sensitive fields.
- Your data is written to a file only when you export it yourself (a full backup in Advanced, your word list, custom phrases or a theme, the log or a heap dump), to a place you choose.
- The exception is the phone-to-phone transfer of Android 12 and later: the system moves the settings, the words learned and added, custom phrases, imported dictionaries and themes to the new phone, but not the clipboard history. The app uses no cloud backup.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Changes to this policy will be made in this file.
